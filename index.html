memory

the idea

you can't smell a screen.

Of all the senses, scent is the one wired straight to memory — it reaches the brain before thought, before language, before you've decided how to feel.

One note can return you to a monsoon afternoon, a grandmother's wrist, a doorway you walked through years ago. That's the quiet power we build around: not a fragrance you wear, but a memory you carry — and leave behind.

every note is a place you've been.

kannauj

the craft · kannauj

a thousand years,
distilled slowly.

We were born in Kannauj — India's ancient perfume capital — and we make scent the way it has been made here for a millennium: the deg-bhapka method. Copper vessels, gentle fire, the patient capture of vapour into sandalwood oil. Slow, low-energy, nothing wasted.

the way it's made is why it stays with you.

  • 1,000 yearsa living craft, not a revival trend
  • low energygentle fire, no industrial shortcuts
  • nothing wastedevery botanical earns its place
